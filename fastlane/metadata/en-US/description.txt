For professionals working on site — capture it, and it's already filed into the project.

Lawyers running due diligence, auditors doing stocktakes, engineers inspecting factories and sites — the photos, video and audio shot on location usually mean exporting, renaming and sorting one by one back at the office. AI WorkDeck Mobile removes that step: shoot on your phone, and each capture is queued and delivered straight into the "Site Media / date" folder of that project on your computer, alongside the rest of the project's files on the desktop app.

A verifiable fingerprint on every capture
A SHA-256 digest is computed the moment a capture is written, recorded together with capture time, GPS coordinates and accuracy, device model and OS version. The digest is generated on device and doesn't depend on a network connection.

Work captures, kept apart from personal photos
Captures live in the app's own library, grouped by project and by day, separate from your personal photos. Whether a copy also goes to Photos is a single switch in Settings.

Nothing lost to a dropped connection
Uploading, staged and delivered are shown as separate states. Losing signal or quitting the app doesn't lose a file — it picks back up when you return.

The cloud is only a relay
The relayed copy is deleted as soon as the desktop app confirms the file has landed on disk; the 7-day expiry is a backstop, not the main mechanism.

Photo, video and audio in one viewfinder
All three capture modes share one screen and file into the same project folder. Audio recording continues in the background. When a call or system event interrupts it, usable audio is saved with a clear notice. Reopen the app after an unexpected exit to see the recovery result.

One phone, every matter you're on
Switch projects and each capture files itself into the right folder.

Apple Watch recording
Start and stop recording on your watch. Recordings automatically transfer to your paired iPhone and follow the existing project upload workflow. Pending recordings stay on the watch while disconnected. Change the project on iPhone before the first upload attempt. Requires watchOS 10 or later.

Who it's for
Due diligence and site visits for law firms, audit stocktakes for accounting firms, supply-chain factory inspections, site acceptance for engineering, insurance surveys, and anyone whose work is document-driven and needs what happens on site brought back into the project.

An AI WorkDeck account and the AI WorkDeck desktop app are required.
